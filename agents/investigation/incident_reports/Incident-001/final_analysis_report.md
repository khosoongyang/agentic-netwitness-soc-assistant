# INVESTIGATION SUMMARY: INC-52825 (Incident-001)

**Final Severity:** High
*Severity is High because the incident involves a non-critical compromised asset with SYSTEM-context execution, repeated suspicious outbound HTTPS communications, and suspicious administrative behavior including UAC-related registry tampering and service-control activity. The presence of lateral-movement indicators and multiple correlated alerts meets the policy threshold for a strongly suspected cyber attack affecting an important endpoint, but available evidence does not confirm widespread compromise, ransomware, or confirmed sensitive data exposure that would require Critical.*

**Confidence Level:** Medium
*Confidence is Medium because multiple reliable telemetry sources align on the same host, user context, suspicious process set, network targets, and administrative tampering indicators. However, evidence is still incomplete for a full process tree and does not conclusively prove the exact malicious spawn chain, persistence, or exfiltration path. Under the evidence sufficiency rules, this is more than a weak suspicion but still not enough for High confidence.*
