# INVESTIGATION SUMMARY: INC-52825 (Incident-001)

**Final Severity:** High
*High severity is warranted because the incident shows repeated suspicious activity on an important workstation with SYSTEM-context execution, suspected lateral movement, service manipulation, UAC tampering, and outbound HTTPS to suspicious external IPs. However, the available evidence does not confirm ransomware, confirmed data exfiltration, or a critical-system outage, so Critical is not supported under Appendix A.*

**Confidence Level:** Medium
*Confidence is Medium because multiple reliable alerts, host/process artifacts, and threat-intel signals align with a malicious interpretation, but the evidence is still incomplete. The key gap is the missing full parent-child process tree and complete log lineage, which prevents definitive confirmation of privilege escalation or exfiltration and aligns with the Appendix F medium-confidence definition.*

## Investigative Workflow
- Reviewed all correlated alerts for INC-50573, INC-51772, INC-52825, and adjacent context from INC-52987.
- Correlated repeated host BETHANYCHUCHU activity across multiple timestamps, users, source IPs, and suspicious process artifacts.
- Identified likely malicious executables and scripts: Sandy.exe, vmtoolsd.exe, WinDefender_Update_2025.ps1, Password_Reset.hta, adduser.msi, and related cmd.exe / sc.exe / reg.exe activity.
- Mapped observed behaviors to suspected lateral movement, service abuse, UAC tampering, and C2-like HTTPS traffic.
- Determined that complete process-tree evidence is missing and that further host-level investigation is required.

## Recommended Containment Actions
- Immediately isolate BETHANYCHUCHU from the network at the EDR layer, preserving management connectivity only for forensics if required.
- Quarantine or block Sandy.exe, vmtoolsd.exe hash 8f490791f7164633e2bc3bfe129c829986a45b918566c2fe1d63f3c77b0eb28c, adduser.msi, and any copied scripts such as WinDefender_Update_2025.ps1 from execution on all endpoints.
- Disable or suspend the newly created local administrative account 'admin2' and verify whether the password Iaminside! was reused elsewhere in the environment.
- Collect volatile evidence from BETHANYCHUCHU before reboot: running processes, network sockets, memory image, autoruns, scheduled tasks, services, and loaded modules.
- Pull full Windows Security, Sysmon, PowerShell Operational, and service-installation logs for 2025-07-02 through 2025-07-14, focusing on Event IDs 4688, 4697, 7045, 4624, 4648, 4672, 5156, and Sysmon 1/3/11/12/13.
- Hunt laterally for the same hash 4a912dc98c977788131aad0ae468d86792211ed225f80b2c344a3690b4437428, vmtoolsd.exe hash 8f490791f7164633e2bc3bfe129c829986a45b918566c2fe1d63f3c77b0eb28c, and connections to 192.168.10.205, 192.168.20.16, 192.168.20.201, 239.255.255.250, and 4.145.79.81 across the enterprise.
- Revert the UAC policy change by restoring HKLM\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Policies\System\EnableLUA to its approved state and verify no additional policy tampering occurred.
- Review VMware Tools and update-related batch files on BETHANYCHUCHU to ensure suspend-vm-default.bat and resume-vm-default.bat were not abused as execution wrappers.
- Block external destinations 40.126.35.151, 52.123.129.14, and 4.145.79.81 at perimeter controls pending full scoping and threat validation.
