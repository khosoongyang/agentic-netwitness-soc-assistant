# INVESTIGATION SUMMARY: INC-52825 (Incident-001)

**Final Severity:** High
*High severity is appropriate because the incident involves a SYSTEM-context endpoint alert, suspected lateral movement, service manipulation, and a registry change that disables UAC (EnableLUA=0). These are strong indicators of adversary activity affecting an important workstation, but the available evidence does not confirm a critical system compromise, ransomware, or verified data exfiltration, so Critical is not supported.*

**Confidence Level:** Medium
*Confidence is Medium because multiple independent indicators align (NetWitness alert, command-line telemetry, internal host-to-host references, and threat-intelligence context), but the evidence is incomplete: the timeline lacks a full process tree, exact remote execution chain, and confirmation of payload execution or exfiltration. Per Appendix F, this is partially sufficient evidence with gaps, not full high-confidence confirmation.*
